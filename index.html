Warming up your account

Warming up means using a new account like anyone else would: watch videos, scroll, like, comment, subscribe and follow channels.

There's no magic number, and it's different on every platform. The best advice is to use an old account, even one with previous uploads. You can always make those private. These are rough starting points.

4 daysYouTube

The quickest of the three to get going.

7 daysTikTok

A full week of normal scrolling and liking.

WeeksInstagram

Some accounts take less, but Instagram is very strict.

What to title your videos

It depends on your niche, but keep titles consistent with your other uploads. Vague ones like "I can't believe this happened" or "This is crazy" look unprofessional and make your video harder to find in search.

Say what's happening

A plain explanation of the video.

Say how to feel

What the viewer should feel about it.

Add a formatter

Optional, like a date or "Part 2".

This Might Be The End Of Roblox Because Of (topic) 😔… | September 7 2026 (date is optional)
Why Did (person) Do (action) To (other person)? 😳… | Part 2 (part number is optional)
